Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Shameer Empowers You ("SEY," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through shameerempowersyou.com, how we use it, and your rights.

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email, phone number, and payment details, when you contact us, join our newsletter, book a session or event, make a purchase in our Shop, or participate in our Community or Forum. We do not sell your personal information. We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

This site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at support@shameerempowersyou.com.
